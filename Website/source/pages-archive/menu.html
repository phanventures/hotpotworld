Some of what rolls by.

Plates ride past your seat under clear lids, every one labelled, so you always know what you are taking.

Vegetarian friendly. Because you pick every ingredient yourself, hot pot is one of the easiest meals to eat around an allergy or a dietary restriction. Just ask your server.

Sliced thin, cooked in seconds.Thịt

Short rib, fatty beef rolls, pork belly. Everything headed for the pot is cut thin enough to cook through almost as fast as you can drop it in, which is the whole trick of hot pot. You are not waiting on a kitchen. You are cooking one bite at a time at your own table.

Thin cuts also let you pace yourself. Take a few slices, cook them, then decide what you want next off the belt.

Eight taps on the wall.Nước Uống

Hot pot is salty by design and the broth keeps reducing while you eat. You will drink more than you planned to.
