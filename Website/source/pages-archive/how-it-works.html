How to eat hot pot.Cách Ăn Lẩu

Nobody is born knowing how to do this. Here is the whole thing, start to finish, so you can walk in already knowing what happens.

Start to finish.

1

Pick your broth

A server will ask what broth you want and walk you through the options. There is no wrong answer, and there is no shame in asking what something tastes like. That is what they are there for.

2

Pick your food

Fresh plates ride past your seat on the belt, each one under a clear lid and labelled, so you always know what you are grabbing. Take a plate. Take six. Keep going as long as you are hungry.

3

Wait, then cook

Your server sets your pot on the burner in front of you and shows you how it works. Give it three to five minutes to come to a rolling boil, then start dropping food in and building your own pot.

Things worth knowing before you sit down.

Everyone gets their own pot. You do not have to negotiate a broth with the rest of your table. Order the one you want.

Thin meat cooks fast. Sliced beef and lamb need a few seconds in a rolling boil, not a few minutes. Dumplings, root vegetables and corn take longer.

Take it slow. There is no rush and no set course order. Cook a little, eat it, go back for more.

Tell us about allergies and diets. Because you pick every ingredient yourself, hot pot is one of the easiest meals to eat around a restriction. Vegetarians can order the vegetable broth and stay entirely in the vegetable, tofu, mushroom and noodle plates.
