Never done this before?

Three steps. That is the whole thing.Cách Ăn Lẩu

Hot pot looks intimidating from the outside. It is not. Your server walks you through it, and you are cooking within five minutes of sitting down.

1

Pick your broth

Your server runs through the options. Rich and porky, clean chicken, sour and spicy Thai, numbing Sichuan, kimchi, or an all vegetable pot. Not sure? Ask. They will steer you right.

2

Grab off the belt

Fresh plates ride past your seat under clear lids, every one labelled. Take whatever looks good. Meat, seafood, greens, mushrooms, noodles, dumplings. Keep taking.

3

Cook and eat

Your pot sits on a burner built into the table. Give it three to five minutes to come to a boil, then drop things in. Thin meat takes seconds. Take your time.

Why "Rotary"

The food comes to you.

Most hot pot restaurants hand you a checklist and a pencil. We run a conveyor belt around the dining room, stacked with fresh plates under clear lids. You see the food before you commit to it, and you never wait on a kitchen.

Every seat gets its own pot and its own burner, so nobody has to agree on a broth. Order what you want, cook it how you like it, at your own speed.

It is a genuinely fun way to eat dinner, and it is the reason people bring out of town guests here.
